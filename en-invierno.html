Prepare your vehicle

Before leaving, get equipped for snow and ice.

  • Snow chains

    Mandatory when road conditions require them. Always carry them and practice fitting them.

  • Check your vehicle

    Tires, brakes, lights, fuel and fluid levels. Carry safety equipment and a first-aid kit.

  • Keep enough fuel

    Keep the tank full in case of delays or problems on the road.

  • Warm clothing and emergency kit

    Carry warm clothes, water, food and basic items: shovel, flashlight and winter essentials.

  • Clear snow from the wheel arches

    Do it regularly to prevent the steering from locking or the vehicle from being damaged.

On the road

Safe winter driving: prevention saves lives.

  • Check road and weather conditions

    Before starting your trip. In winter, conditions change within minutes.

  • Drive at a cautious speed

    Respect limits and adapt your speed to the road and weather conditions.

  • Keep a safe distance

    Increase it: on snow or ice you need much more space to brake.

  • Headlights on

    Keep them on at all times during the whole trip.

  • Avoid driving at night

    And during the late afternoon, when the temperature drops and ice forms.

  • Slow down on curves

    To prevent running off the road or rolling over.

  • No sudden maneuvers or sharp steering

    Brake gradually and steer smoothly and progressively.

  • Do not use cruise control

    Never on snowy or icy roads.

  • Slow down when passing other vehicles

    This prevents stones, mud, ice or water from being thrown and makes overtaking easier.

  • Clear windshield, rear window and lights

    To ensure good visibility at all times.

  • Caution: loose animals

    Wild animals may be on the road or the shoulder. Pay special attention and drive at a prudent speed.

If something happens on the road

What to do in case of a breakdown or an accident.

  • Do not stop on the road

    Unless strictly necessary. If you must stop, use the shoulder or a safe place.

  • In case of a breakdown

    Mark the area and stay inside the vehicle with your lights on (hazard and fog lights).

  • In case of an accident or problem

    Report it to the authorities, giving your location and the number of occupants.

  • Follow instructions

    From security personnel and road agencies, and obey traffic rules.
