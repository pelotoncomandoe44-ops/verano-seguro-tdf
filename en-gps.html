Get back to a town

If you are lost: if you do not know the terrain, the safest thing is to stay where you are, keep warm and give your coordinates by phone or radio. The direction to a road or town is only a reference: in a straight line there may be rivers, peat bogs, dense forest or cliffs.

Schematic map available offline: main roads are approximate. Use it to orient yourself; MAPS.ME (offline, with the map downloaded) or Google Maps (with data) give the exact route.

Sorted from nearest to farthest. Choose which app opens the route:

Get your phone ready before you go:
  1. Install MAPS.ME – Free navigation app that works without internet.
  2. Download the Tierra del Fuego map – Open MAPS.ME, search for "Tierra del Fuego" and tap Download. With that map you can navigate by car or on foot with no signal.
Android · Google Play iPhone · App Store
Google Maps needs mobile data unless you downloaded the area. MAPS.ME navigates with no signal if you downloaded the map beforehand. In remote areas, the walking option is only for short distances: in an emergency, call or use the radio and wait for help.
